# Gap Analysis Guide

## What is a Gap?

A **gap** occurs when a stock's opening price is significantly different from the previous day's closing price. This creates a visual "gap" on the price chart—an empty space with no trading occurring between those two prices.

### Why Do Gaps Occur?

Gaps typically result from significant events that occur after market hours:
- **Earnings announcements** (positive or negative surprise)
- **News announcements** or company developments
- **Analyst upgrades/downgrades**
- **Economic data** releases
- **Market-wide events** or overnight news

---

## Types of Gaps

### 1. **Gap Up** ⬆️
**Definition:** Today's opening price is **HIGHER** than yesterday's closing price.

**Example:**
```
Yesterday:  Closed at $100
Today:      Opened at $103
Gap:        $3 or 3% gap up
```

**What it means:**
- Indicates positive overnight sentiment or news
- Buyers outnumbered sellers overnight
- Stock attracted strong demand after hours

**Typical action:**
- May continue higher (momentum play)
- May pullback/consolidate intraday
- Often reverses if it's an "exhaustion gap"

---

### 2. **Gap Down** ⬇️
**Definition:** Today's opening price is **LOWER** than yesterday's closing price.

**Example:**
```
Yesterday:  Closed at $100
Today:      Opened at $97
Gap:        -$3 or 3% gap down
```

**What it means:**
- Indicates negative overnight sentiment or news
- Sellers outnumbered buyers overnight
- Stock lost strength/faced selling pressure

**Typical action:**
- May continue lower (capitulation move)
- May bounce/recover intraday
- Often reverses if it's an "exhaustion gap"

---

## When is a Gap "Closed"? (Gap Fill)

A gap is considered **"closed" or "filled"** when the price returns to the previous day's closing price.

### Gap Up Close Example:
```
Gap UP from $100 to $103
Gap closes/fills when price drops back to $100
```

### Gap Down Close Example:
```
Gap DOWN from $100 to $97
Gap fills when price rises back to $100
```

### Why Gaps Fill:
- Traders who missed the overnight move try to enter at better levels
- Profit-taking when gap shows weakness
- Consolidation and mean reversion
- Support/resistance at the gap closing price

---

## Gap Types by Characteristics

### **Common Gaps** 📍
- Small, fill quickly (within hours or a day)
- Usually low volume
- Not very significant for trading
- Common in low-volatility stocks

### **Breakaway Gaps** 🚀
- Large gap with high volume
- Signals start of new trend or breakout
- Often don't fill for a long time (weeks/months)
- Bullish: occur at start of uptrend
- Bearish: occur at start of downtrend

### **Runaway Gaps (Continuation Gaps)** 📈
- Occur mid-trend, continuing strong momentum
- High volume
- Don't fill quickly
- Signals trend strength

### **Exhaustion Gaps** 🔄
- Occur at END of a strong trend
- Often followed by reversal
- Last attempt at momentum before reversal
- Frequently fill quickly (often same day)

## Using Gaps in Your Trading

### **Gap Up Trading Setup:**
1. **Find stocks:** Use "Up Gap %" filter to find gap-up stocks
2. **Assess strength:** Higher % gap = stronger overnight move
3. **Watch for:**
   - **Continuation:** Stock stays above yesterday's close → trend is strong
   - **Pullback:** Stock pulls back to yesterday's close → potential entry for longs
   - **Reversal:** Stock gaps back through yesterday's close → trend failed

### **Gap Down Trading Setup:**
1. **Find stocks:** Use "Down Gap %" filter to find gap-down stocks
2. **Assess weakness:** Higher % gap = stronger overnight selling
3. **Watch for:**
   - **Continuation:** Stock stays below yesterday's close → trend is weak
   - **Recovery:** Stock bounces back to yesterday's close → potential short entry
   - **Reversal:** Stock rises through yesterday's close → trend failed

---

## Filter Settings Explained

### **"Up Gap %" and "Down Gap %" Fields**
These show the **percentage** of the gap from previous close.

**Example Settings:**
- `Up Gap % > 3`: Find stocks that gapped up more than 3%
- `Down Gap % > 2`: Find stocks that gapped down more than 2%
- `Up Gap % between 2 and 5`: Find gap-ups between 2-5% (moderate gaps)

**Interpretation:**
- **Larger gaps** = More significant overnight move
- **Smaller gaps** = Minor overnight move
- **No filter** = All gaps (including tiny ones)

---

## Gap Pullback Analysis

The **"Gap Pullback Level"** shows Fibonacci retracement levels of the gap:

### **Fibonacci Levels:**
- **38.2%:** Shallow pullback → momentum likely continues
- **50.0%:** Mid-level pullback → balanced setup
- **61.8%:** Deep pullback → near complete fill, strong support/resistance
- **78.6%:** Full pullback → gap essentially filled, major reversal level

### **Example:**
```
Gap up $10 from $100 open to $110 theoretical high
- 38.2% pullback: Price pulls back to $103.82
- 50.0% pullback: Price pulls back to $105.00
- 61.8% pullback: Price pulls back to $106.18
- 78.6% pullback: Price pulls back to $107.86 (near gap fill at $100)
```

## Trading Tips

1. **Larger gaps require more space to fill** - Don't expect them to fill quickly
2. **High volume on gap = stronger signal** - More conviction behind the move
3. **Gap at support/resistance = more significant** - Price already at key level
4. **Exhaustion gaps** are most likely to fill - Watch for them near trend ends
5. **Breakaway gaps** rarely fill - Don't expect quick reversal
6. **Use gaps with other signals** - Combine with volume, support/resistance, trend

---

## Common Trading Mistakes

❌ **Don't:** Assume all gaps fill  
✅ **Do:** Classify the gap type first (common, breakaway, runaway, exhaustion)

❌ **Don't:** Trade gaps without confirming volume  
✅ **Do:** Check volume to confirm strength of the gap

❌ **Don't:** Ignore the trend direction  
✅ **Do:** Trade gaps in the direction of the larger trend

❌ **Don't:** Hold losing gap trades too long  
✅ **Do:** Set stop losses at previous close or key levels

## Example Watchlist Values

In your stock list, you'll see:
- **GAP %**: Shows the actual gap percentage
- **LEVEL**: Shows which day's setup (not the Fib level, but the gap occurrence)

Use these to:
1. **Sort by largest gaps** - Find most significant overnight moves
2. **Filter by gap direction** - Find only gap-ups or gap-downs
3. **Compare gap sizes** - Rank by strength of overnight sentiment
4. **Track gap fills** - Monitor which gaps are filling intraday
